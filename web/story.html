The pattern holds.

Two dips and a rebound, every year, for a decade. Whatever else happens to NYC — weather, policy, fare hikes — Jul 4th, early September and year-end show up on the chart in the same shape.

These aren't the only calendar effects. The overlay also shows smaller recurring dips around Memorial Day and Thanksgiving, plus quieter signatures from the spring holidays — hover any point to see the day's value across years.

The weekly rhythm degraded.

Three equal-length windows: the 78 days before the PAUSE, the lockdown itself, and the first 78 days of COVID re-opening. Before shows the normal Mon-Sun shape; during is near-flat, near-zero; after is partial — not back to before. The shape itself is what changed.

A long, uneven climb back.

Demand begins to recover in summer 2020 and continues through 2021–22, but the trajectory is jagged and well below the pre-pandemic line for years. The pulse returns; the calendar does not, quite.
